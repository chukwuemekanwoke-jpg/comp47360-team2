# Tablé User Research Evidence Package

This directory contains the proposed public and anonymised evidence for Tablé's exploratory research, internal product review, and formative usability evaluation. It supports the shorter method and findings presented in the group paper while excluding raw material that could identify participants.

## Participant accounting

The two participant-based rounds contain **seven distinct people**:

- E01--E03: three early exploratory student interviewees; and
- P01--P04: four later formative usability evaluators, comprising one internal evaluator and three external student peers.

The early interviewees did not return for the later usability round. Round 2 involved five team members in one internal review session (`IR01`) and is not added to the user-research participant count.

## Evidence boundary

The package is based on:

1. facilitator recollection of three short exploratory interviews;
2. a team action summary produced after internal testing and demo review; and
3. the private P01--P04 usability report dated 24--26 July 2026.

Because no Round 1 transcript was retained, its detailed answers are explicitly labelled as retrospective paraphrases. They document how the team reconstructed its design reasoning but are not presented as exact quotations. Persona content is a design synthesis, not an additional participant record.

## Publication gate

Before this directory is moved into the public repository, the team must:

1. confirm that the participant counts, findings, and implementation-status wording match the final paper;
2. approve the directory-specific restrictive licence;
3. complete the privacy audit in [`PRIVACY_AND_ANONYMISATION.md`](PRIVACY_AND_ANONYMISATION.md).
